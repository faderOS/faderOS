vMix

Shared PST and PGM mappings, stored by identifier to survive vMix input renaming or reordering.

Connect vMix under faderOS → Server.

PST selects preview; PGM switches program directly. CUT swaps buses. AUTO TRANS uses the mappings below and TRANS RATE in milliseconds, independently of the four vMix transition buttons. Unassigned effects are never executed. FTB uses the native vMix duration.

ATEM

Shared PST and PGM mappings. Sources are discovered from the device. The two physical banks have 12 positions each.

Connect ATEM under faderOS → Server.

PST selects preview; PGM switches program directly. CUT and AUTO use the selected M/E layers. TRANS RATE uses frames (1–250). NEXT TRANS selects BKGD and keyers; double-click BKGD selects background only. WIPE, DME and T-bar depend on device capabilities.
